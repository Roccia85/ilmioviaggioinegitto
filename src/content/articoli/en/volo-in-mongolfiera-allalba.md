There's a moment, just before dawn, when Luxor holds its breath. The sky shifts
from the deep blue of the Nile to gold, and dozens of balloons rise slowly above

## What time you leave

The wake-up call is early: hotel pick-up is around 4:30. You cross the Nile by
boat to the launch point on the west bank.

- Flight duration: about 45 minutes
- Best season: October to March
- What to bring: a light sweater, your camera

An experience you simply don't forget.
